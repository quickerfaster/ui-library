## One addition I'd make

I would also create a document in your repository called:

```text
docs/architecture/application-platform-blueprint.md
```

and keep this prompt synchronized with it.

That document becomes the **single source of truth** for the platform's architecture. Whenever you start a new AI thread, you can paste the latest version or reference it. It also benefits future collaborators (or even your future self) because architectural decisions are recorded outside of chat history.

I think this practice will save you a lot of time as your framework grows and will greatly reduce the risk of "AI context drift" across long-running design sessions.
